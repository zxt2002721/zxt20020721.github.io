About

I am a CS graduate student at Northeastern University (Seattle). Broadly, I am interested in language models and interactive systems for healthcare: how to capture patient behaviors (adherence, lifestyle, conversations), how to summarize them reliably, and how to design workflows that clinicians can trust and control.

Previously, at the Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences (CASIA), I worked on vascular health prediction using physiological signals for diabetic foot research.
